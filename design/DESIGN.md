## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, institutional, and architectural aesthetic tailored for elite real estate markets and verified property intelligence. Rejecting superficial gold leafing or ornamental excess, the identity embraces an architectural and institutional rigor anchored by deep maritime depth and sharp rectilinear precision.

### Target Audience & Psychological Response
- **Audience:** High-net-worth investors, institutional funds, regulatory bodies, and discerning property buyers navigating high-value UAE residential and commercial transactions.
- **Emotional Response:** Uncompromising structural trust, legal certainty (Trakheesi/RERA/Madmoun compliance), architectural permanence, and technological modernity.

### Visual Style
- **Movement:** Architectural Modernism fused with Technical Constructivism.
- **Execution:** Zero-radius geometry, crisp technical hairline boundaries, structural grid lines, and monospaced data density contrasted against authoritative sans-serif typography. Surfaces convey physical permanence rather than ephemeral software layers.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to balance state-level regulatory verification with high-tier private wealth and architectural weight.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#0F766E` / Creek Teal):** Serves as the core trust anchor, signaling government validation, active Trakheesi permits, and certified ownership titles. Used for primary interactive triggers, active verification states, and focal metrics.
- **Secondary (`#0B1F3A` / Gulf Midnight):** Provides structural mass. Used for heavy architectural headers, foundational structural frames, primary typography, and authoritative night-mode contrasts.
- **Tertiary (`#C8A165` / Desert Gold):** Used sparingly as an accent for ultra-prime property indicators, premium structural callouts, and curated selection tags. It must never overwhelm informational utility.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937` / Charcoal):** High-legibility base for body copy, secondary containers, and technical data grids.
- **Surface & Canvas Accents:**
  - `Base Canvas`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `Dune Sand` (`#F6F1E9`): Used for secondary programmatic sections, filter rail backdrops, and document preview zones.
  - `Mist` (`#E5E7EB`): Strict 1px tectonic divider lines.
  - `WhatsApp Green` (`#25D366`): Dedicated transactional broker connection channel.

### Application Rules
- Maintain pure `#FFFFFF` backgrounds for data-dense and legal verification sheets.
- Dune Sand (`#F6F1E9`) is restricted to structural banding and alternating layout modules to ground spatial volume without introducing decorative clutter.
- Verification badges must pair Creek Teal with `#FFFFFF` or dark deep marina layers to maintain immediate contrast.

## Typography

The typographic system fuses the engineered geometry of `Space Grotesk` with the balance and legibility of `Manrope`.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headlines & Structural Titles (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers technical authority and rectilinear precision. Headlines feel drafting-table accurate, conveying master-planned property developments and regulatory clarity.
- **Body & Data Prose (`Manrope`):** High-performance legibility for property specs, legal disclaimers, valuation narratives, and cross-border purchase criteria.
- **Numbers, Prices, & Trakheesi Permit Data:** All currency amounts (AED) and license registration numbers must enforce tabular figure sets (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "zero" 1`) to guarantee vertical column alignment across comparative matrices.

### Localization & Bilingual Layout
- When rendering Arabic language content, type styles map to `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` maintaining equivalent proportional scale, optical density, and weight distribution.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a precision-first 12-column grid system derived from an 8px base rhythm. Layouts convey balance, wide architectural vistas, and clear data indexing.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container: `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`. Content stacks to a single logical axis; side-by-side elements strictly limited to numeric key-value pairs (e.g., Bed / Bath / Area metrics).

### Spacing Discipline
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Layouts leverage deliberate macro-spacing (`space-xl` and higher) between major thematic zones (e.g., Hero search to Certified Registry listing), paired with compact micro-spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) inside property attribute tables and verification badges.
- **Zero-Bleed Layout Boundaries:** Surfaces sit edge-to-edge separated by crisp hairline boundaries rather than floating irregularly across arbitrary gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain technical authenticity, depth is generated through tectonic layering, surface tonal shifts, and ultra-sharp hairline structural framing rather than blurry drop shadows.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F6F1E9` (Dune Sand). The absolute structural floor.
- **Tier 1 (Framed Containers & Cards):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces delineated by 1px solid `#E5E7EB` borders. Absolutely no diffuse shadows. Separation occurs strictly via line-work and tonal shifts.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Headers, Navigation, & Flyouts):** Solid `#FFFFFF` or `#0B1F3A` with a hard, technical hairline perimeter line (`1px solid #16345F` or `#E5E7EB`). If physical elevation is required for floating search bars over satellite map layers, utilize a directional, razor-sharp architectural drop: `box-shadow: 0px 4px 0px 0px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Verification Drawers):** High-contrast structural overlays flanked by a dark, semi-translucent field (`rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.6)`), bounded by sharp 1px borders in Creek Teal (`#0F766E`) or Deep Marina (`#16345F`).

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

### Geometry Rules
- **0px Corner Radius:** Every component—from primary interaction triggers and text entry fields to large property imagery cards, verification seals, and modal overlays—features 90-degree corners.
- **Architectural Meaning:** Sharp rectilinearity conveys permanence, blueprint engineering, and institutional validity. Curves soften and diffuse boundaries; sharp edges align with blueprint drafting guidelines, cadastral land parcel plots, and Dubai's iconic structural skylines.
- **Image Treatment:** Property photography must always be cropped with sharp right angles, encased within a 1px `#E5E7EB` frame or flush against neighboring content grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Compliance / Action):** Solid `#0F766E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 0px border-radius, font `Space Grotesk` uppercase tracking (0.04em). Height: 48px. Hover: shifts to `#0B1F3A` instantly with no soft easing curves.
- **Secondary (Institutional / Corporate):** Solid `#0B1F3A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 0px border-radius.
- **Tertiary / Wireframe:** 1px solid `#0B1F3A` or `#E5E7EB`, transparent background, dark text.
- **WhatsApp Broker Contact:** `#25D366` solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, paired with sharp icon glyph.

### Cards & Property Tiles
- Background `#FFFFFF` encased in a strict `1px solid #E5E7EB` frame.
- **Property Visuals:** Edge-to-edge media at the top, followed by an internal inset padding of `1.25rem`.
- **Metadata Matrix:** Floorplan area, bedroom count, and bath figures must be divided by vertical 1px hairline rules.
- **Price Display:** Rendered in `Space Grotesk` 600 weight, using tabular figures: e.g., `AED 4,850,000`.

### Verification & Compliance Badges (Trakheesi / RERA / Madmoun)
- Compact, rectangular badges with a 0px radius.
- Background: `#0F766E` (or 10% tint `#0F766E1A` with a 1px `#0F766E` border).
- Label: `Space Grotesk` 11px uppercase bold with a verified icon glyph and official permit number tracking.

### Inputs & Filters
- Flat `#FFFFFF` fields framed with a 1px solid `#E5E7EB` border.
- **Active / Focus:** 1px solid `#0F766E` border with an immediate inner 1px offset, maintaining zero corner rounding. Labels sit above fields in small uppercase `Space Grotesk`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Exact 18x18px squares, 0px radius, 1px border `#1F2937`. Checked state: solid `#0F766E` background with a crisp geometric white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Exact 18x18px outer square with a centered 8x8px square indicator for the selected state.

### Lists & Cadastral Tables
- Alternating rows with `#FFFFFF` and `#F6F1E9` backgrounds.
- Headers framed in `#0B1F3A` with `#FFFFFF` text in `Space Grotesk`. Dividers rendered in `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
